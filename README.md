## The entry

`main.ts` writes a single sentence (222 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> On summer evenings my grandmother lifted the lid off the rice pot, steam fogging her glasses as she pretended not to see me scraping the crispy bottom with my fingers, and I have never waited for dinner the same way since.

A summer evening in grandmother's kitchen: the smell of fresh rice, steam on her glasses, a small theft she pretended not to see — and the warm ache of never waiting for dinner that way again.
